One neuron can change who the model thinks it is

Add a fixed number to one MLP neuron of Qwen3-8B, and it answers "What are you?" with "I am the Lord… the Alpha and the Omega", or as Napoleon, or as a nature poem.

This map shows where those neurons are. Each vertical sheet is one layer (0 to 27, input on the left). Each dot is one of its 12,288 neurons.

  • Steered neurons. We pushed them up (+) or down (−) and read the replies.
  • Persona score. How much of a figure's persona a neuron carries, for the figure you pick at the top. A high score is a hint, not proof.

Click a dot to read its replies. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, click a layer to see it face-on.
